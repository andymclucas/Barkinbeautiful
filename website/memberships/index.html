Memberships

Grooming, on a subscription.

Most salons sell one groom at a time and hope the client rebooks. A membership books the dog every few weeks and bills a small amount each week. You get steady income and a full diary. The dog never goes too long between grooms.

Income you can plan on

Weekly payments arrive whether it's a busy week or a quiet one, so you know what's coming in before the month starts.

A diary that fills ahead

Members are booked on their rhythm, months ahead, so the gaps you fill each week get smaller.

Better-kept dogs

Regular grooms mean fewer matted coats and fewer shave-downs, which is easier on the dog and on your groomers.

How it works

From sign-up to the first groom.

  1. The owner signs upOn your membership page they choose a plan and save a card through Stripe.
  2. You approveNothing is charged and nothing is booked until you've looked at the request and said yes.
  3. Booked on rhythmThe dog is booked every 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 weeks, depending on the plan.
  4. Billed each weekA small weekly amount is charged to the saved card, automatically.

The details that matter

Built for the awkward cases too.

  • The groom is covered, the extras aren't. Add-ons done on the day go on an invoice as normal.
  • Declined card? Retried automatically on the next business day, with an accounts list showing who still owes.
  • Going on holiday? Pause the membership and pick it up again later.
  • Monthly figures done right. Worked out on 52 weeks a year, not four weeks a month, so the numbers add up.

Questions

About memberships.

Which plan includes memberships?

Memberships come with Software + Messaging and with Complete. Every free trial has them switched on.

Who holds the members' card details?

Stripe. Owners enter their card on Stripe's secure page, and card numbers never reach Groomigo or your salon.

Can an owner sign up without me?

They can send a request from your membership page, but nothing is charged or booked until you approve it.

What if a member's card is declined?

The payment is tried again on the next business day, and it shows in your accounts list so you can follow up if it fails again.
