The floor

The salon floor, live.

Grooming software usually ends at the booking. Groomigo follows the dog through the building: who is in the bath, who is on the dryer, who is on the table, and who is ready to go home.

Checked in→ Bath→ Dry→ Groom→ Ready

Without it

"Who's next in the bath?"

Shouted across the salon, or worked out from a whiteboard nobody updated.

Without it

"Is she ready yet?"

The fourth call this morning, answered with wet hands from the front desk.

Without it

"Why were we late again?"

Nobody knows whether it was the bath, the dryer or the table that held the day up.

The board

Every dog in the building, on one screen.

One tap moves a dog to its next stage, and everyone sees it: bathers, groomers, the front desk, the screen on the wall and the owner's phone.

Twelve states

Each wait is tracked as well as the work.

A dog waiting for the dryer isn't the same as a dog on it. Groomigo records both, which is how it can tell you where the day went.

Arrive

Booked, then checked in when the dog comes through the door.

Bath

Waiting for bath, ranked in the queue, then bathing.

Dry

Waiting for the dryer, then drying.

Groom

Waiting for a table, then grooming with their groomer.

Ready

Ready for collection, then complete once they've gone home.

Exceptions

Cancelled and no-show, kept apart so they never skew your timings.

Where the day went

Find what's holding the day up.

The timing report breaks each day into its stages and its waits. If dogs are queuing for the dryer, you'll see it, and you'll know whether another dryer or a different start time would fix it.

  • Time at every stage, for every dog, recorded as it happens.
  • Waits shown separately from the work itself.
  • Day by day, so a pattern shows up rather than a one-off.

The owner's side

The same moment, on the owner's phone.

When a dog moves on the board, its tracker moves too. Owners see their dog's progress without ringing, and the pick-up text goes out with one press when the dog is ready.

  • No app, no login. A link that opens on any phone.
  • Updates by itself every 30 seconds.
  • Call the salon in one tap if they need to.
