Switching

Moving salons is hard. Moving software shouldn't be.

Groomigo's own salon moved across from MoeGo while it was open and booked out. We know what goes wrong in a move because we've done one, and the process below is how we'd want to be moved.

Coming from MoeGo

We move you across.

You don't export and re-import spreadsheets. We bring your records over for you and check them with you before you switch.

  • Clients and their dogs, with contact details.
  • Upcoming bookings, so the diary is ready on day one.
  • Visit history, so you can still see past grooms.
  • Handling alerts and weights, so nothing a groomer needs is lost.

From anything else

Send us your client list.

Coming from another system or a spreadsheet? If it can export a spreadsheet, we bring your clients across as part of setup. Or import them from a CSV file yourself.

  • Clients imported from a CSV file: names, phone numbers and emails.
  • Duplicates found and merged, so the list starts clean.
  • Seven days free to set everything up before you pay.

How a switch goes

No gap in the diary, no lost clients.

  1. Start a trialSign up and tell us from inside Groomigo that you're switching.
  2. We set you upYour records come over, your services and staff are set up, and you check it all against what you know.
  3. Run side by sideKeep your old system going for a few days while the team gets comfortable.
  4. Switch overMove the front desk across on a day you choose. Your diary is already there.

The honest part

Saved cards can't be moved. We make re-collecting them easy.

A saved card belongs to the payment company that saved it, so no software can carry cards from one system to another.

  • One text to everyone who needs a card on file, each with their own secure link.
  • Cards go straight to Stripe. You never see or type a client's card number.
  • The same goes for agreements: a personal link to sign on their phone.

Questions

About switching.

Will my clients notice the move?

Only in good ways. Their bookings are already in Groomigo on the day you switch. The one thing they'll be asked for is a card, if you keep cards on file, by a text with their own secure link.

Do I lose my history?

No. Coming from MoeGo, your past visits come across with your clients and dogs, so groomers can still see what was done last time.

Can I try Groomigo before deciding to switch?

Yes. The seven-day trial has every feature switched on and needs no card. Look around first, then ask us to bring your records across when you're ready.

What if I'm not on MoeGo?

If your current system can export a spreadsheet, send it to us and we bring your clients across as part of setup. You can also import a CSV file yourself.

What does setup cost?

A one-off fee, set by your plan: $500 for Software, $750 for Software + Messaging and $990 for Complete, all plus GST. It pays for us to move you across and set you up. Founding salons pay no setup fee. What setup includes.
