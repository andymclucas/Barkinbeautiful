Our story

Built on a grooming floor, not in an office.

Groomigo started inside Barkin' Beautiful, a busy Australian grooming salon. The software we had kept the diary, and nothing else. So we built the rest.

The diary was never the hard part.

Booking a dog in takes a minute. The hard part is the day itself: a building full of dogs, bathers and groomers handing them along, and owners ringing to ask if their dog is ready. The software we used couldn't see any of that. It knew when a dog was booked and when it was paid for, and nothing in between.

So we put the floor on a screen.

We built what the floor needed. A workflow board: every dog, every stage, one tap to move it on. A bath queue, because bathers kept asking who was next. A tracker, because owners kept ringing. Memberships, because regular clients deserved a better deal than rebooking one groom at a time.

Every feature earns its place on a real floor first.

Groomigo runs Barkin' Beautiful every day. When the team finds something that slows them down, they report it from inside the app, and it gets fixed. That's how it got this good, and it's how it keeps getting better.

What we believe

The rules we build by.

The dog comes first

A failed payment never switches a salon off. There are dogs in the building, so we ask for a new card and the salon keeps running.

Your clients are yours

Export your client list whenever you like, on any plan, even after you leave. We will never hold your data to keep you.

No card numbers, ever

Cards are handled by Stripe. Card numbers never reach Groomigo or your salon.

Plain words

Screens, texts and prices written the way a groomer talks. No jargon, no hidden fees.

Made for Australia

Australian dollars, GST on every invoice, Australian spelling, and the salon's own time zone everywhere.

Proved before it ships

Every change is tested before it reaches a salon, and the big ones are tried on a working floor first.
