The diary

A diary that knows what a groomer's day can hold.

Day, week and agenda views with a column for every groomer. Drag a booking to move it. Groomigo works out how long each dog takes and warns you before a groomer's day goes over their limit.

  • Family bookings for several dogs, on one invoice.
  • Recurring visits every 1 to 14 weeks, ending after a number of visits, on a date, or never.
  • One-tap rebook from today's visit.
  • Next available finds the first free slot for you.
  • Leave and blockouts close a groomer's diary, online booking included.
  • Booking warnings for blocked clients and dogs that need care.
  • Undo for a booking deleted by mistake.

The floor

The live workflow board.

The part of Groomigo you won't find elsewhere. Every dog on the board, moving through check-in, bath, dry, groom and ready, with the time spent at each stage. See the floor in detail.

Ranked bath queue

Dogs waiting for the bath are ranked next, after next and so on, in colour, so bathers never have to ask who is next.

Stage timers

Each dog shows how long it has been at its current stage. A long wait stands out before it becomes a late pick-up.

Where the day went

A timing report of the waits between stages, so you can see whether the bath, the dryer or the table is holding the day up.

TV display

Put the board on a screen on the salon wall. It refreshes itself and runs on the salon's own clock.

Walk-ins and families

Add a walk-in from the board. Dogs from one family are grouped and can be checked in and sent home together.

My day, for groomers

Each groomer has their own view of their dogs for the day, where they move stages and add photos.

For owners

An experience owners talk about.

Every owner-facing page carries your salon's name. Most open from a simple link, with no app to download and no password to remember.

Live pet tracker

A link that shows where the dog is up to, from checked in to ready, updating by itself every 30 seconds, with a button to call the salon.

Client portal

Owners see upcoming visits, reschedule into a time you have free, cancel, pay, save a card and message the salon. It installs on their phone like an app.

Report cards

A before-and-after photo slider, a note from the groomer and what was checked over. Happy owners are invited to leave a public review.

One-tap quotes

Found matting, or the owner wants a different style? Send a quote for the extra work. They approve or decline from their phone.

Agreements by text

Your service agreement, signed from a text link or in the portal, so it is done before the first visit, even for owners with no email.

Send us a photo

A link owners use to send a photo of their dog, with no login, straight onto the dog's record.

Online booking

Bookings that respect how your salon works.

A public booking page where owners choose a groomer from their profiles, see a price estimate and request a time. Only times that fit the groomer's day are offered, and you confirm each request.

  • Size and weight checks, so a groomer is only offered for dogs they take.
  • Capacity respected, including each groomer's daily limit and leave.
  • Waitlist owners join themselves. When a spot frees up, you send them a link to request it.

Messaging

Every text in one inbox. Every call accounted for.

Texts and email to clients, from your salon, with your team sharing one conversation history.

Two-way texting

One inbox for every client conversation: threads, starring, read state, search, and failed sends flagged so nothing is missed.

Missed calls handled

An alert when the phone rings, a list of missed calls, voicemail written out as text, and one reply texted to a missed caller.

Reminders that act

Booking confirmations and reminders four days, two days and the morning before. A client who replies N cancels their own booking.

Pick-up texts

One press when the dog is ready, and the owner knows to come.

Campaigns

Email campaigns to the clients you choose, filtered by who they are and how recently they came.

Good manners built in

Opt-outs honoured, texts held until after 9am, and a running count of the texts you have used.
