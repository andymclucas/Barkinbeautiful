Questions

About plans and billing.

Do prices include GST?

No. Prices are in Australian dollars and exclude GST, which is added to your invoice.

What happens when the trial ends?

Choose a plan any time during the seven days and you carry straight on. If the trial ends without a plan, the salon pauses until you choose one. Nothing is deleted, and you can still sign in, pay and export your client list.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Change plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel from the billing page, whenever you like.

Why is there a setup fee?

Because we move you across rather than leaving you to do it. Your clients, dogs, bookings, services and staff are brought into Groomigo and checked with you before you switch. The fee is charged once. Salons that pay yearly, and founding salons, pay none.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Software is $1,150 a year, Software + Messaging $2,110 and Complete $3,550, all plus GST. Paying yearly costs a little less than twelve months, and there is no setup fee.

Is the price per groomer?

No. Each plan is a price per salon.

What if we send more texts than the plan includes?

Groomigo counts the texts your salon sends and warns you as you get close to the month's allowance, so it's never a surprise.
