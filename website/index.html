The workflow board

Know where every dog is, without walking out the back.

Each dog moves through twelve tracked states, from booked to collected. The board shows them as the floor really works, with the time each dog has spent at every stage.

  • Where the day went. A report on how long dogs waited for the bath, the dryer and the table.
  • On the wall. A display mode for a TV, refreshing itself on the salon's own clock.

And everything a salon needs

Everything you'd expect, done properly.

Groomigo covers the whole working day, not only the bits that demo well.

Online booking

Owners request a time online, with groomer profiles, size checks and a price estimate. Only times that fit the groomer's day are offered.

Two-way texting

One inbox for every client text, missed-call alerts, voicemail transcripts and a reply sent once to a missed caller.

Reminders that act

Reminders four days, two days and the morning before. A client who replies N cancels their own booking.

Payments

Card payments through Stripe, cards on file, split cash and card, store credit, discounts and counter sales.

Clients and pets

Handling alerts, style notes, weight history, vaccinations, photos, and a duplicate finder that merges records cleanly.

Waitlist

Owners join the waitlist themselves, and you offer a freed slot with a link they tap to request it.

Who's overdue

A list of dogs overdue for a groom, worst first, judged against each dog's own visit rhythm.

Reports

Revenue, groomer productivity, retention, lapsed clients and what they cost you, with targets per groomer.

Questions

The things salon owners ask first.

Can I move my clients across from MoeGo?

Yes. We move your clients, dogs and booking history across for you, and check it with you before you switch over. You don't have to export and import spreadsheets yourself. How switching works.

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. Sign up with your salon's name, your name, an email and a password. You get seven days with every feature switched on, and you choose a plan when you're ready.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Groomigo runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and installs to the home screen like an app. Groomers have their own "My day" view of their dogs, and the board works on a TV on the salon wall.

Who holds my clients' card details?

Stripe does. Cards are entered on Stripe's secure page, and card numbers never reach Groomigo or your salon.

What happens if my subscription payment fails?

Nothing is switched off. You have dogs in the building, so we ask you to update the card and the salon keeps running.

Can I get my data out?

Always. You can export your client list at any time, on any plan, even after you stop paying. Your clients are yours.
